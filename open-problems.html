Open Problems ⫽ Record Tables

This collection brings together open-problem lists and record tables related to elliptic curves, rational points, Diophantine tuples, Mordell–Weil rank, torsion, and explicit arithmetic geometry. The goal is to maintain a practical set of research targets: problems that remain unresolved, constructions whose known records may be improved, and questions that are suitable for computational or structural attacks.
