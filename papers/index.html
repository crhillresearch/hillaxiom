Two Rational Families of Elliptic Curves with Trivial Torsion and Generic Rank at Least Eighteen

Abstract. I give two rational base changes of a rank-17 elliptic K3 fibration. Each produces an elliptic curve over ℚ(u) with 18 independent sections and no rational torsion. The coordinates, height matrices, and Sage checker are in the companion Rank Ledger entry. I also compare the parent fibration with Elkies’s 2026 rank-17 example. The section-lattice determinants have a nonsquare ratio, so the two elliptic fibrations cannot be identified by a rational Möbius change of parameter. This ties the known generic rank bound of 18. It does not raise it.

Nineteen Independent Sections from Two Quadratic Covers of an Elliptic K3 Surface

Abstract. I give two explicit quadratic covers of a rank-17 elliptic K3 fibration. Each cover adds a section; on their genus-one compositum, the seventeen inherited sections and the two new ones are independent. The parameter curve has a rational point of infinite order. This yields infinitely many elliptic curves over ℚ of rank at least 19, and the fibers can be chosen to have trivial rational torsion. The exact section coordinates and a SageMath verification script are available in the companion repository. The numerical bound C(0) ≥ 19 was already known; this is a different explicit construction.

Regularity-Selected Generic Rank at Least Four in the Piezas Diophantine Sextuple

Abstract. We study the one-parameter Piezas rational Diophantine sextuple over ℚ(t). Its regularity identities select six of the twenty triple classes; for each selected class, four explicit rational sections are proved independent by specialization at t = 12/5 using rigorous canonical-height Gram certificates, yielding generic Mordell–Weil rank at least four.
